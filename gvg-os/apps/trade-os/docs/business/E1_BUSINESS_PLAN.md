# E1 Business Plan — GVG Global Trade OS

**Edition:** E1 (Executive / Entry plan)  
**Company:** Global Vista Group  
**Product:** Global Trade OS  
**Purpose:** Concise plan for leadership alignment and early investor / partner conversations

---

## 1. Opportunity (one page)

Cross-border SME and mid-market buyers still run sourcing on email, spreadsheets, and disconnected freight/customs tools. That creates slow RFQ cycles, weak supplier comparison, and surprise landed costs.

GVG Global Trade OS unifies **discover → RFQ → quote → order → ship → clear → settle** with role-based portals and an AI Procurement Assistant.

## 2. Solution

Enterprise SaaS Trade OS with 20 platform modules spanning marketplace, trade, WMS/logistics, CRM, CMS, analytics, AI, and admin.

**Core commercial offers**

1. Platform subscription (Buyer / Supplier / Enterprise tiers)  
2. Transaction / success fees on facilitated orders (optional later)  
3. US Purchasing & consolidation service margin  
4. OEM/ODM program management fees  
5. Premium AI / compliance add-ons  

## 3. Beachhead

- US-bound / US-origin purchasing programs  
- Health supplements, household, hardware, apparel OEM  
- Distributors and brand owners who already run multi-supplier import programs  

## 4. Business model (E1)

| Stream | Model | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS seats | Monthly/annual per company + roles | Near-term |
| Marketplace take-rate | % of GMV or flat order fee | After liquidity |
| Services | US purchasing / sourcing retainers | Near-term |
| Add-ons | AI pack, customs OCR, ERP connectors | Phase 2–3 |

## 5. Go-to-market (E1)

1. Founder-led sales to existing trade relationships  
2. Supplier onboarding to seed catalog liquidity  
3. Content + SEO/GEO for category landing pages  
4. Pilot cohorts (5–20 accounts) with white-glove RFQ support  

## 6. Traction plan (12 months)

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| Q1 | Auth + persistent RFQ/quote/order; 3 design partners |
| Q2 | WMS/logistics MVP; 10 paying seats / pilot fees |
| Q3 | AI matching in production; first US purchasing cohort |
| Q4 | Repeat GMV on platform; Series-seed metrics package |

_Numeric revenue targets should be finalized by finance before investor use._

## 7. Team needs (E1)

- Product / full-stack (platform)  
- Trade operations lead (RFQ, customs literacy)  
- Supplier success / catalog QA  
- Later: sales, finance ops, compliance advisor  

## 8. Funding ask (placeholder)

Use of funds categories:

1. Product engineering (Auth, trade cycle, WMS, AI)  
2. Compliance & trade advisory  
3. Supplier/buyer onboarding  
4. Cloud infrastructure & security  
5. GTM runway  

_Ask amount and cap table: fill before external sharing._

## 9. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Marketplace cold start | Seed with owned/partner catalogs + services revenue |
| Customs liability | AI suggests; humans confirm; clear ToS |
| Trust / quality | Supplier verification workflow |
| FX / payments complexity | Phase FX providers; start USD-first |

## 10. Why now

- AI makes RFQ drafting and matching usable for SMEs  
- Mid-market still underserved vs enterprise ERP suites  
- Fragmented post-pandemic supply chains reward orchestration platforms
