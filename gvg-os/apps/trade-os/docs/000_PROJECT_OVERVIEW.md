# 000 — Project Overview

**Product:** GVG Global Trade OS  
**Company:** Global Vista Group (GVG)  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Architecture:** Enterprise SaaS  
**Tagline:** Connect. Source. Ship. Scale.

---

## Vision

GVG Global Trade OS is an enterprise-grade B2B and B2C international trading platform connecting suppliers, manufacturers, buyers, logistics providers, and global sourcing services into one operating system for cross-border commerce.

## Problem

Global trade teams juggle fragmented tools for sourcing, RFQ, quoting, ordering, warehousing, logistics, customs, CRM, and finance. That fragmentation causes slow cycle times, compliance risk, and poor landed-cost visibility.

## Solution

A unified Trade OS that covers marketplace discovery through supply-chain execution, with role-based workspaces and an AI Procurement Assistant.
