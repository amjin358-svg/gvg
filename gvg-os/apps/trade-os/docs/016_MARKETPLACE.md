# 016 — Marketplace

## Scope

Public and authenticated commerce discovery surfaces.

## Modules

- Home
- Products / Product detail
- Categories
- Brands
- News
- CMS (content for marketplace)

## Product verticals

Health Supplements · Packaged Foods · Household Goods · Hardware & Tools · Home Improvement · Furniture · Branded Apparel · OEM/ODM

## Key product attributes

- SKU / slug
- Brand + origin country
- MOQ + unit price + currency
- Lead time
- Compliance / program tags (GMP, OEM, UL, etc.)
- Stock vs made-to-order

## Buyer journeys

1. Browse category → product → Start RFQ
2. Search via AI assistant → matched SKU → RFQ draft
3. Read news/insight → related procurement CTA

## Supplier journeys

1. Onboard brand/products in Supplier Portal
2. Respond to RFQs with quotes
3. Maintain lead time & MOQ accuracy

## Trust & quality

- Verified supplier badges (Phase 2)
- Documented certifications on product/brand
- Audit trail for price changes
