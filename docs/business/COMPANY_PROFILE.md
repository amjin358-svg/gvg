## 1. Who we are

Global Vista Group (GVG) builds and operates an enterprise international trading platform that connects suppliers, manufacturers, buyers, logistics providers, and global sourcing services in one operating system.

We focus on cross-border commerce where fragmented tools create delay, compliance risk, and opaque landed cost — especially across OEM/ODM, US purchasing, and multi-vertical merchandise programs.

## 2. What we offer

**GVG Global Trade OS** — a B2B/B2C Trade Operating System covering:

| Layer | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| Marketplace | Products, categories, brands, news/CMS |
| Trade cycle | RFQ → Quote → Order |
| Procurement | Global sourcing, US purchasing, OEM/ODM |
| Supply chain | Inventory, warehouses, logistics, customs docs |
| Relationships | CRM for buyers & suppliers |
| Intelligence | AI Procurement Assistant |
| Control | Role-based admin, analytics |

## 5. Operating model

- **Product:** Multi-tenant-ready SaaS Trade OS (enterprise architecture)  
- **Services:** Platform-enabled trading, US purchasing support, sourcing programs  
- **Delivery:** Cloud (Vercel + Supabase + Cloudflare), role-based portals  
- **Quality bar:** Production-ready UX, WCAG AA, SEO/GEO readiness, strict TypeScript engineering standards  

## 6. Differentiators

1. **Trade OS, not a single-point tool** — marketplace through customs in one system  
2. **Role-native workspaces** — guest to super admin, including AI agent role  
3. **AI-assisted procurement** — matching, RFQ drafts, landed-cost estimates  
4. **Vertical + corridor awareness** — supplements to apparel, US purchasing included  
5. **Enterprise engineering discipline** — docs, RBAC, repository pattern, CI
