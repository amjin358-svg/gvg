## Slide 1 — Title

**Global Vista Group**  
GVG Global Trade OS  
*Connect. Source. Ship. Scale.*  
Enterprise Trade Operating System for cross-border commerce

## Slide 2 — Problem

Global trade teams drown in fragmented tools:

- Email RFQs and spreadsheet quote comparisons  
- Disconnected inventory, freight, and customs packets  
- Late discovery of true landed cost  
- No shared system of record across buyer / supplier / ops / finance  

**Result:** Slow cycles, compliance risk, lost margin.

## Slide 3 — Insight

Mid-market importers and brand owners need an **operating system**, not another single-purpose marketplace tab or a multi-year ERP rollout.

## Slide 4 — Solution

**GVG Global Trade OS** — one platform for:

Discover → RFQ → Quote → Order → Inventory/Warehouse → Logistics → Customs → CRM → Analytics  
+ **AI Procurement Assistant**

## Slide 6 — Who pays

| Buyer | Why |
|---|---|
| Distributors / importers | Speed + landed-cost clarity |
| Brand owners | OEM/ODM program control |
| Suppliers | Demand + quote workflow |
| Enterprise trade desks | Role governance + auditability |

## Slide 7 — Business model

1. **SaaS subscriptions** (primary)  
2. **Trade-enabled services** (US purchasing, sourcing programs)  
3. **Take-rate / order fees** (as liquidity grows)  
4. **AI & compliance add-ons**

## Slide 8 — Why now

- AI finally usable for RFQ drafting + supplier matching  
- Supply chains remain multi-sourcing and corridor-specific  
- Mid-market stuck between DIY tools and heavy ERP  

## Slide 9 — Moat thesis

- Workflow depth across trade + ops (hard to clone as a thin marketplace)  
- Role graph + auditability for enterprise trust  
- Vertical catalog + corridor playbooks (US purchasing, OEM)  
- Data network effects from RFQ/quote/order history + embeddings  

## Slide 10 — Go-to-market

Design partners → seeded suppliers → US purchasing wedge → content/SEO → partnerships (forwarders, inspectors)
